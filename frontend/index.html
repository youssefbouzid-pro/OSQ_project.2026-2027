Getting Started

Learn how CloudyBTTounsi labs work.

What is CloudyBTTounsi?

CloudyBTTounsi gives you temporary, isolated lab environments for Linux, database administration, and DevOps practice.

How labs work

  1. Choose a lab from the catalog.
  2. Wait for the environment to become ready.
  3. Connect to the lab using Tailscale SSH.
  4. Complete the lab tasks.
  5. Find and submit flags to prove completion.

Important rules

  • Each lab lasts 60 minutes.
  • Labs cannot be extended.
  • When time expires, the lab is destroyed automatically.
  • Do not store important data inside the lab.

Access Setup

Set up Tailscale to connect to your lab environments.

Install Tailscale

Install Tailscale on your laptop and sign in with your lab account.

Verify your connection

tailscale status

Connect to a lab

tailscale ssh student@linux-beginner-12345.cloudybt-tounsi.ts.net

Troubleshooting

  • If the lab does not appear, wait until its status is Running.
  • If SSH fails, check that Tailscale is active on your device.
  • If the lab expired, start a new session.

Help & FAQ

Common questions and answers.

How long does a lab last?

Each lab lasts 60 minutes. The countdown starts when the lab becomes ready.

Can I extend my lab time?

No. Labs have a fixed TTL. If you need more time, start a new lab.

What is a flag?

A flag is proof that you completed a task. Flags are placed inside the lab environment by the scenario.

What happens when the lab expires?

The lab instance is terminated automatically and its data is removed.

I cannot connect to the lab.

Check the Access Setup page, verify Tailscale is running, and make sure the lab status is Running.
